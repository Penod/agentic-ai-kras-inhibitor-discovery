# NIW Positioning Draft

## Proposed Endeavor

Develop agentic AI systems for KRAS mutant-selective anti-cancer drug discovery, focusing on computational screening, evidence synthesis, and molecular validation for pancreatic cancer and lung adenocarcinoma.

## Substantial Merit

The endeavor addresses cancer drug discovery, precision oncology, computational biology, and AI-enabled biomedical innovation.

## National Importance

KRAS-driven cancers are a major health burden, especially pancreatic cancer and lung adenocarcinoma. KRAS was historically difficult to drug, but recent clinical progress around KRAS G12C shows that computational and translational research can help expand therapeutic options.

## Applicant Positioning Evidence To Build

- A working open-source KRAS screening platform.
- A reproducible ChEMBL/BindingDB/RDKit/XGBoost pipeline.
- Documented benchmark results and model cards.
- A technical report or preprint describing the system.
- Cloud-native deployment and audit logs.
- Portfolio screenshots, diagrams, and demo recordings.
